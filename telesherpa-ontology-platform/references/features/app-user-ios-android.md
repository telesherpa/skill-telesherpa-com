# App users (iOS & Android)

The Telesherpa Ontology Platform can also be used via native iOS and Android apps — your
human colleagues can do everything on mobile with them: fill in forms, take photos
and upload them to the structured image store, accept and complete ServiceOrders,
view objects on site. What an agent creates or triggers via API/MCP shows up
directly for humans in their app — and vice versa.

## App vs. web

- **Configuration** (scopes, forms, image store categories): web only.
- **App**: consumer — reads and operates, does not configure.

For an agent itself nothing changes as a result — it always works via MCP, regardless
of whether the human end user sits at the phone or at the desktop.
